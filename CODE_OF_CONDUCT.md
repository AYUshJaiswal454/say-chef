# Code of Conduct

Say Chef is a project about sharing food, and kitchens work best when everyone feels welcome in them. This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being welcoming to newcomers and patient with questions
- Respecting different cooking traditions, tastes and opinions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and recipes posted to any Say Chef deployment run by the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow them.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/AYUshJaiswal454). All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Depending on severity, maintainers may respond with a private warning, a temporary ban from interacting with the project, or a permanent ban.
